[trax platform overview, v7.11, February 2025](https://www.bio-stream.ca/_files/ugd/9bd49f_a78a3570e8e84f1197c57c0bac957c52.pdf).

The [current homepage](https://www.bio-stream.ca/) identifies the reader as under
development and not yet approved as a medical or diagnostic device.
The proof-of-concept headline is **See results for [target] with trax™.**
This is original, user-facing copy for the imagined experience after buying and
using a reader, not a verbatim quotation or evidence of commercial availability.
The targets above remain research examples; the overview does not establish
that these are approved tests available for purchase.
This source supports the selected examples; it does not establish readiness for clinical use.

The reviewed sources did not substantiate the previous A1C, allergy, or STI labels.
That is a limitation of the material reviewed, not a claim that such development is impossible.

Bio-Stream describes electrochemical sensing, so the scene copy now refers to
electrochemical signals rather than an optical path.

Both product calls to action use the requested [all-products page](https://www.bio-stream.ca/category/all-products).
